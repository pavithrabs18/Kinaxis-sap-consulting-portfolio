# US Chemical Industry: End-to-End Kinaxis Demand Planning

**Role:** Demand Planning Maestro consultant, Accenture Strategy and Consulting (2025 to present)

## Context

A US chemical manufacturer implementing demand planning on Kinaxis RapidResponse, including a migration from an existing Kinaxis environment.

## What I did

- Implemented and supported the end-to-end demand planning solution.
- Configured statistical forecasting, consensus demand processes, and S&OP workbooks.
- Validated master data, historical sales, and forecast outputs against expected results.
- Built small test datasets to check forecasting logic, overrides, and what-if scenarios.
- Analyzed existing Kinaxis configuration and documentation to define migration requirements and identify risks.
- Broke high-level project tasks into detailed JIRA sub-tasks for integration, architecture, and operations teams, and tracked them to completion.
- Acted as the main client liaison, resolving cross-team dependencies and issues as they arose.

## Skills used

Demand planning, Kinaxis configuration and validation, scenario analysis, requirement analysis, JIRA, stakeholder management.
