# Private Cloud Operations for Japanese Clients

**Role:** Assistant technical service engineer, Fujitsu Consulting India (Dec 2019 to Apr 2024)

## Context

Operations support for a private cloud platform on VMware, working daily with Japanese clients and teams. Two assignments: the Fujitsu Local Cloud Platform (Aug 2020 to Aug 2022) and monitoring of a cloud-based data store (Aug 2022 to Apr 2024).

## What I did

- Deployed and changed virtual machines (CPU, memory, disk) to client requirements.
- Ran monthly Windows updates, OS checks, and VM provisioning and removal.
- Worked on network configuration involving load balancers and switches.
- Handled ServiceNow tickets and monitoring through Slack, and fetched logs from vCenter, Tera Term, and network devices.
- Performed vMotion and Storage vMotion and other tasks from SOPs and workarounds.
- Coordinated hardware replacement work and vendor support desks, with mail coordination between vendors and clients.
- Received KT from the Japanese client and transferred it to team members.

## Recognition

Continuous Star awards in every year at Fujitsu.
