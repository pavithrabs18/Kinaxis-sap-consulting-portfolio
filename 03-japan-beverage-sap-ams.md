# Japanese Beverage Company: SAP AMS Support

**Role:** Bilingual technical support analyst, Accenture (May 2024 to Nov 2024)

## Context

An application management services engagement for a leading beverage company in Japan, covering the SAP SD order-to-cash process from transition through go-live and stabilization.

## What I did

- Supported order-to-cash processes through transition and go-live.
- Generated and analyzed sales order, delivery, and billing data, and ran regular system health checks to keep data accurate.
- Delivered knowledge transfer sessions on functionality and business processes for onboarding and continuity.
- Acted as the English and Japanese coordinator in client meetings, workshops, and issue resolution.
- Translated functional documents, user guides, and reports between English and Japanese.
- Shadowed SAP SD functional work: organizational structure, master data, schedule line category determination, standard and special sales processes.

## Skills used

SAP SD, AMS support, KT delivery, health checks, bilingual coordination.
